## How to resume

This is a multi-session document. To resume: read the **Progress Tracker**, find the first row that is not `✅ Done`, and continue from its `Resume from:` marker. Any change here propagates forward to `cv.md` and `cv.yaml`.

Rules of the interview: one question at a time; always offer a recommended answer to react to; never invent facts or metrics (mark estimates `~` and list under **To verify**); every keyword gets context.
